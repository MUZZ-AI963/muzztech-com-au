How it holds

Share what they know.
Keep who decides.

MUZZ sits between interconnected AI systems. It is designed to let them cooperate without letting one take over another’s judgement.

Information passes

Observations, data, evidence and context are admitted, so cooperation keeps its value.

Dictation is refused

Instructions or judgements meant to set another system’s decision are refused before they are admitted.

The attempt is recorded

Each refused attempt is kept in a tamper-evident record, so people can investigate and revoke the agent.

Works alongside security

It complements identity, authorisation and cyber fundamentals such as the Essential Eight. It doesn’t replace them.

Connect the intelligence without connecting the judgement.
